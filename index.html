01 / The Program

B.S. Computer Science
CS Online

The CSUMB Computer Science Online program is a fully online bachelor's degree built for transfer students. Across two years and thirteen courses it moves from programming foundations through software design, operating systems, databases, networking, algorithms, and security, and finishes with a year long senior capstone. The program is organized around Major Learning Outcomes in software development, computing systems and theory, and the social and ethical context of technology.

Program Outcomes · what I can do now

  • → Design and build full stack and native applications in Java, JavaScript/Node, React, and SQL.
  • → Reason about systems: operating systems, computer networks, and security.
  • → Apply algorithms, data structures, and formal logic to real problems.
  • → Work on an Agile team with version control, code review, testing, and deployment.
  • → Weigh the social, ethical, and accessibility impact of the software I build.

Goals

My goal is to build software at the intersection of healthcare and technology. I spent years in cardiac care and now work in QA at a medical device company, and I have seen how often the people who need technology most are the ones it leaves behind. After I graduate in Fall 2026 I want to move fully into software engineering and work on systems that are reliable, secure, and usable by everyone, not just the people who already have the best tools. My capstone, MediTap, is the first step in that direction.
