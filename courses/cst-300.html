CST 300 · Spring 2025

Major ProSeminar

Course Description

Helps students identify and articulate personal, professional, and social goals. Provides an integrated overview of the computer science and communication design majors and their requirements. Students develop a plan for their learning goals and build writing, presentation, research, and critical thinking skills within the diversified fields of information technology and communication design.

What I Learned

CST 300 set the foundation for the whole program. I worked on articulating my goals as a computer science student and learned how to research and argue a position in writing. My main deliverables were a two part AI Companionship video, one cut for a general audience and one for technologists, and an ethics argument paper on whether it should be legal to hack back. It was my first real practice at writing and presenting technical ideas for different audiences.
