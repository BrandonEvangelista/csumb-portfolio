Capstone — MediTap

Course Description

Plan and deliver a substantial software project. CST 489 covers the proposal, requirements, and design; CST 499 covers building, testing, and presenting the finished product.

What I Learned

My capstone is MediTap, a medication adherence platform that brings together my cardiac and healthcare background with everything I learned in this program. Patients tap an NFC sticker (or scan a QR code) on a prescription vial to log a dose, and a caregiver gets a read only view. Writing the proposal taught me to scope a solo project to a real 10 week timeline and to plan usability testing with real people from the start.

Evidence

Final project: MediTap, an NFC based medication adherence tracking platform.
