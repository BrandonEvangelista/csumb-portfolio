CST 462S · Spring 2026

Race, Gender, Class in the Digital World

Course Description

Examines how race, gender, and class shape access to technology and who benefits from it, paired with service learning and a team research paper on a real social and technology issue.

What I Learned

This class made me look at tech from the outside in. Our team researched how taking part in open source software communities affects economic mobility for people from low income backgrounds, which hit close to home since I contribute to LibreOffice myself. I also reflected on my professional identity as a software engineer and what I owe the people who use what I build. Coming from healthcare, where access gaps are real and visible, I want the software I work on to be usable by everyone, not just the people who already have the best tools.
