Reasoning with Logic

Course Description

Formal reasoning for computer science. Covers propositional and first order logic, translating English into logic, and building natural deduction proofs, the same skills that underpin program correctness, databases, and AI.

What I Learned

I learned to build natural deduction proofs step by step in a proof checker, including how proof by contradiction really works: to prove something, assume its negation and drive it to a contradiction inside the subproof. In first order logic I worked with quantifier rules, translated English sentences into formulas, and defined reflexive, symmetric, and transitive relations from scratch. It changed how I read requirements and test conditions at work, since a test case is really just a logical claim about the system.
