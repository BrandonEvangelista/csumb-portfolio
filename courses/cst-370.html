CST 370 · Spring 2026

Design & Analysis of Algorithms

Course Description

Design and analysis of algorithms. Covers hashing, heaps, graphs, and trees, sorting and searching, and design strategies including brute force, divide and conquer, decrease and conquer, transform and conquer, dynamic programming, and greedy methods, with a focus on efficiency.

What I Learned

Recursion was the hardest part for me at first, especially finding the base case and tracing the calls by hand. Once that clicked, merge sort and quicksort made sense as divide and conquer, and I learned why median of three pivots protect quicksort from its worst case. I learned how AVL trees rotate to stay balanced, how heaps and hashing work, and how dynamic programming saves work by storing answers. I completed weekly programming assignments in Java and C++ through GitHub Classroom, including the coin row problem and Floyd's all pairs shortest paths.
