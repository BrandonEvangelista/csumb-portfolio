CST 328 · Spring 2026

Digital Art and Design

Course Description

Visual design principles applied to digital media: image editing, game and interface design, and a full user centered app design process from wireframes to a working prototype in Figma.

What I Learned

Going in, I didn't think much about design beyond whether it works. This class changed that. I optimized images in Photoshop, redesigned a game menu, and built a level in a game design activity. The main project was NutriLog, a nutrition logging app I took through the full design process: medium fidelity wireframes, high fidelity wireframes, an interactive Figma prototype, and a final presentation, with peer critique each round. I learned mobile conventions like fixed bottom navigation and why showing design progression matters as much as the final screens. As a QA engineer, it gave me a much better eye for usability issues.
